# 02 · Super Hearing — prompts

**Context:** You joined Rook two weeks ago as PM on Dispatch.
Release 4.2 shipped on 12 August, just before you arrived, and
landed badly. Last session you built a context file and met the
company.

You still do not know what actually went wrong.

At the end of the session, ask Claude Code to save the prompts you
wrote yourself below — not the starter prompt. The closing slide has
the exact prompt to paste. By Module 6 this file is a prompt library
built from your own questions.

---

### 1.

The way this is presented is hard to digest. help me organize this into some artifact so I can better understand what signal I should focus on first and then the others later. And as you are creating this artifact, I want it presented to me at a 5th grade level.

### 2.

Help me finish this sentence

Based on the interviews, the trouble with 4.2 is ___.

### 3.

to close out the session, what updates should we make to the repo?

### 4.

Which of these problems appears to have the biggest impact on responders, and what evidence from the interviews supports that conclusion?

### 5.

You say going quiet for weeks may be more harmful even though only 2 of 4 handlers mentioned it. What evidence supports that conclusion, and what evidence contradicts it?

### 6.

If you only use the four interviews and ignore the tickets, data, and code, what conclusion about Release 4.2 can you defend with the strongest evidence? What conclusion would you avoid making because the interviews don't support it well enough?

### 7.

Why is going quiet much more common in the support tickets than it was in the interviews? What evidence in the tickets could explain that difference?

### 8.

If we count unique responders instead of individual tickets, what problems are actually most widespread, and does that change what we should conclude from the tickets?

### 9.

Using only the support tickets, is there evidence that going quiet and offers vanishing are connected, or could they simply be two separate problems affecting the same responders?

### 10.

Only look at tickets filed after August 12. What problems appear after that date that weren't present before, and what can the tickets actually prove changed versus what would require another source to verify?

### 11.

When the interviews and tickets disagree, which source should I trust for each type of claim, and why? Use specific examples from both folders.

### 12.

How much of the disagreement between the interviews and tickets could be explained by who was included in each group rather than by the sources actually contradicting each other?

### 13.

If we combine the interviews and tickets without assuming either source is more representative, what conclusions are supported by both sources, and what conclusions are supported by only one?
