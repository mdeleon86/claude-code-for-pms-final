# 03 · Rewind — prompts

**Context:** You joined Rook two weeks ago as PM on Dispatch.
Release 4.2 shipped on 12 August, just before you arrived, and
landed badly.

Last session you read four conversations and every support ticket
since 4.2 — and found the two piles did not agree.

At the end of the session, ask Claude Code to save the prompts you
wrote yourself below — not the starter prompt. The closing slide has
the exact prompt to paste. By Module 6 this file is a prompt library
built from your own questions.

---

### 1.

For the four responders whose pings collapsed after 4.2, calculate each person's average weekly pings before the release and after the release separately. Show me the rows and calculations for each person so I can verify the size of the drop.

### 2.

If we exclude the four responders whose pings collapsed, what happened to the other 12 responders after 4.2? Calculate their before-and-after weekly pings and acceptance rate, show me the rows behind the calculation, and tell me whether the release still appears to have affected them.

### 3.

How sensitive are these conclusions to how we treat the week of August 10, since Release 4.2 shipped in the middle of that week? Recalculate the key results with the release week excluded, counted as before, and counted as after. Tell me which conclusions remain true in all three cases.

### 4.

What else does the data tell us about why those 4 have never recovered?

### 5.

Which tickets don't match the timing in the data? For each one, show me what the person reported, when they reported it, and what their actual ping history shows around that date.

### 6.

Do the 18 tickets that conflict with the data have anything in common? Look for patterns in who filed them, when they were filed, and what they describe that could explain why the tickets and the data are telling different stories.

### 7.

Given the contradictions between the tickets and the data, what conclusions about the 4.2 release are supported by both sources and which conclusions depend on trusting only one source? Show me the evidence for each so I know what I can confidently report.

### 8.

Follow Vesper through August week by week. Show me how many pings she received and how many she took each week, then explain in plain English how her experience changed over the month.

### 9.

Based only on Vesper's weekly data, is there evidence that missing more pings led to receiving fewer pings the following week? Walk me through the sequence and tell me what the data supports versus what we'd still be assuming.

### 10.

Compare Vesper with responders like Sgt. Bulwark who also missed a lot of pings during release week but did not lose future pings. What was different about their pattern that might explain why Vesper entered the downward spiral while they recovered?

### 11.

If I had to explain to Vesper why his pings dropped while Bulwark's didn't, what information are we still missing? Rank the missing evidence by which would do the most to confirm or rule out the possible causes, and explain what each would tell us.

### 12.

i want you to administer 5 agents, one to explore each area here. i want these agents to debate each other on their findings, and then align on where they feel is the root-cause to all of this. if they cannot align on that, at least help me understand what information they need to better understand how we solve this?
